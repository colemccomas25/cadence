## Section 3: How it works (3 steps)

**H2:** Set up your studio in under 10 minutes.

1. **Add your students.** Paste from your spreadsheet — name, parent email, instrument, default rate. Done.
2. **Schedule recurring lessons.** Click an empty slot. Pick the student. Pick "weekly" or "biweekly." Cadence does the rest, including holidays.
3. **Get paid on the 1st.** Cadence emails parents a Stripe invoice for last month's lessons. They click → pay. You see "$1,840 collected this month" on your dashboard.

**Visual:** Three small screenshots in a row — student form, weekly calendar, dashboard with payment total.

## Section 7: FAQ

Six questions. Use the actual objections you'll hear.

1. **Can I import my students from a spreadsheet?**
Yes. Upload a CSV with name, parent email, instrument, and rate — Cadence handles the rest.

2. **What happens if I have 7 students on the free plan?**
You won't be charged automatically. We'll let you keep using Cadence with your first 5 students active and put the rest in a "waitlist" status until you upgrade or archive someone.

3. **Do you charge fees on payments?**
No. You pay Stripe's standard 2.9% + 30¢ directly — Cadence takes nothing on top. (This is rare in our category.)

4. **Will you raise prices on me later?**
Your monthly price is locked for as long as you stay subscribed. New tiers and price changes only apply to new customers.

5. **What about make-up lessons / cancellation policies?**
Cadence supports the most common rules: free 24h+ cancel, charge for late cancel, credit make-up to next invoice. You set the policy per student.

6. **Can I get my data out?**
Always. Export students, lessons, and invoices to CSV at any time. No lock-in.

## Copy principles I followed

- **No "AI-powered" or "revolutionary."** Music teachers are skeptical of tech bro language.
- **Specific numbers:** "$1,840 collected" not "more revenue." "24 hours" not "soon."
- **Problem-first, not feature-first.** The hero leads with the pain.
- **Founder voice.** Solo SaaS sells better with a real person attached.
- **Concrete CTAs.** "Start free — up to 5 students" beats "Get started" because it tells you what happens next.
