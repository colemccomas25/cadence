## 1. Indie Hackers post

**Title:** I built studio management software for private music teachers in 9 weeks — here's what I shipped

**Body:**

Nine weeks ago I watched a close family friend manage 22 piano students in a Google Sheet that broke every time the school year started. The existing tools — you know the ones — all promised "studio management" and delivered software that needed studio management.

So I built Cadence.

**What it does:**

- Recurring weekly lessons on a calendar (not "round robin booking")
- Monthly invoices automatically emailed to parents via Stripe on the 1st
- 24-hour lesson reminders so you're not texting parents yourself
- Family billing — one invoice for two siblings, no math
- Make-up lesson tracking
- CSV import for your existing roster

**Stack:** Next.js 15, Neon Postgres, Drizzle ORM, Stripe, Resend, Vercel. ~9 weeks of part-time work.

**What I learned:**

The hardest part wasn't the code — it was resisting the urge to build features nobody asked for. I cut group lesson scheduling, a student portal, and a mobile app. The thing that remained is the thing music teachers actually do every week.

Early signal: [X beta users], [Y lessons scheduled], [Z invoices sent].

The free plan supports up to 5 students. Solo plan is $19/month.

**Link:** cadence.app

Happy to answer any questions about the stack, the niche, or the build process.
